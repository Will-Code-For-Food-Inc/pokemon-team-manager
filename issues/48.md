---

### Problem Statement
The move summaries in the knowledge base (KB) are not prominently surfaced when queried, particularly when asking the LLM questions such as "What moves can [Pokémon name] use?" While general knowledge is retrieved effectively, move-specific data does not seem to be prioritized or as accessible as it could be.

---

### Requirements

#### Functional Requirements
1. **Enhanced Contextual Surfacing:**
   - Ensure move summaries are treated as high-priority data when querying the knowledge base.
   - Implement mechanisms to display summaries for specific moves first without requiring users to dig through general knowledge.

2. **Query Rewrite or Scoring Update:**
   - Modify either the query interpretation or result-ranking aspect of the LLM to prioritize moves.
   - Examples:
     - Query: "What moves can Pikachu use?"
       - Expected Result: Focus on Thunderbolt, Quick Attack, etc., with short summaries and key stats directly presented.

3. **Format and Presentation Updates:**
   - Design a visually-structured format for move summaries.
   - Include the following:
     - Move name.
     - Type (e.g., Electric, Fire, etc.).
     - Power and Accuracy (with unit labels).
     - A brief description.

#### Non-Functional Requirements
1. **Efficiency:**
   - The updated system should retrieve and surface move summaries within a query response time of less than X seconds.

2. **Scalability:**
   - Ensure that the system performs consistently across a large knowledge base with hundreds or thousands of entries.

#### Additional Considerations
- Accessibility:
  - Ensure move summaries are readily available via voice input methods as well, maintaining clarity.
  
- Localization:
  - Consider requirements for future multi-language knowledge base expansion.
