# Pokemon Champions Team Building Guide (Regulation M-A, April 2026)

## Format Fundamentals

### Stat Points (NOT EVs/IVs)
- 66 total stat points per Pokemon, max 32 per stat
- All Pokemon of same species start identical — allocation is the differentiator
- Cannot reach "0 Speed IV" floor for Trick Room; must rely on naturally low base Speed
- VP cost: 2 VP per stat point (see CLAUDE.md for full VP costs)

### Item Scarcity
Missing vs. standard VGC: Assault Vest, Life Orb, Choice Scarf/Specs, Weakness Policy, Loaded Dice.
This forces role compression — each Pokemon must cover multiple roles since you can't itemize out of weaknesses.

## Team Archetypes

### 1. Tailwind Hyper Offense
**Core:** Whimsicott (Prankster) + fast physical/special attackers
- Prankster guarantees priority Tailwind regardless of Speed matchup
- Best against Trick Room teams lacking backup speed control
- Build 5-6 individually strong members, adapt through team preview
- Whimsicott moveset: Tailwind, Encore, Moonblast, Protect

### 2. Hard Trick Room
**Core:** Hatterene + Indeedee-F
- Indeedee-F's Psychic Surge blocks Fake Out — the #1 Trick Room disruption tool
- Hatterene sets TR; naturally low base Speed (55) with 0 stat point allocation
- Heavy hitters: Torkoal (Eruption), Rhydon, Cofagrigus, naturally slow attackers
- Weakness: requires naturally slow base Speed Pokemon; stat points alone insufficient

### 3. Mega Froslass Snow Offense (63.8% win rate)
**Core:** Mega Froslass + Incineroar + Hatterene/Dondozo
- Mega Froslass gains Snow Warning on evolution → 5-turn snowstorm
- Snow: Blizzard 100% accuracy + 50% Defense boost for Ice-types
- Aurora Veil (50% damage reduction) requires snow to set
- Early April meta lacks reliable weather removal — this wins by default in many matchups

### 4. Rain
**Core:** Pelipper (Drizzle) + Basculegion / Archaludon
- Hurricane 100% accurate in rain
- Archaludon: Electro Shot gains +1 SpA without charge turn in rain
- Add Tailwind for early speed before rain kicks in

### 5. Sand
**Core:** Tyranitar + Excadrill (56.2% win rate)
- Excadrill's Sand Rush doubles Speed in sand
- Garchomp unaffected by sand chip damage

### 6. Sun
**Core:** Mega Charizard Y (Drought) or Torkoal
- Venusaur (Chlorophyll) doubles Speed in sun
- Torkoal's Eruption hits hardest at full HP under Trick Room + sun

### 7. Balance / Goodstuffs (most-played by representation)
- No single win condition; 5-6 strong independent Pokemon
- Flexible 4-of-6 preview selection is the win condition
- Requires overlapping coverage across all 6 slots
- Defensive pivots (Rotom variants, Corviknight) + reliable offense (Incineroar, Garchomp)

## Speed Control Hierarchy

1. **Priority moves** — Fake Out (Incineroar, Sneasler), Sucker Punch (Kingambit)
2. **Tailwind** — Whimsicott (Prankster, guaranteed priority)
3. **Trick Room** — Hatterene + Indeedee-F (blocks priority disruption)
4. **Weather speed boosts** — Sand Rush, Chlorophyll, Swift Swim
5. **Stat point allocation** — high-Speed mons allocate elsewhere; low-Speed mons allocate to offense

Fake Out chains (Incineroar + Sneasler together) create repeatable 2-turn tempo control.

## Core Synergy Principles

### Role Compression
Each Pokemon needs 2+ roles. Sinistcha = Rage Powder redirect + Hospitality healing + special bulk. One slot, three jobs.

### Weather Setters Are Overcentralized
Missing items (Assault Vest etc.) mean no defensive workarounds. Weather override is rare — if you set weather, you often keep it.

### Preview Flexibility
Build 6 Pokemon with overlapping coverage so any 4-of-6 can handle common archetypes. Rigid 2-mon cores are exploitable through preview.

### Stat Point Allocation Philosophy
- Naturally fast Pokemon (Talonflame, Sneasler): put points in bulk/offense
- Naturally slow Pokemon (Hatterene, Torkoal): put points in damage output
- Mid-Speed (Incineroar, Garchomp): allocate based on your speed strategy

## Common Phantom Engine-Style Builds

Ghost/Fire offense with speed control (Tailwind or Trick Room support) is a proven archetype:
- Ghost attackers (Ceruledge, Golurk) threaten Normal-immune damage
- Fire coverage (Ceruledge Bitter Blade, Incineroar Flare Blitz) handles Steel-types
- Gardevoir provides Fairy/Psychic special pressure
- Whimsicott Prankster Tailwind enables outspeeding without Speed investment
- Dragonite Multiscale + ExtremeSpeed provides priority cleanup

Key weakness of this archetype: Ghost-type moves hit your own Ghost-types. Position carefully.
