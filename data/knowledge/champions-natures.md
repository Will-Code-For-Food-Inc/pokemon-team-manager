# Stat Alignments (Natures) in Pokemon Champions

## Overview

Pokemon Champions uses 21 valid Stat Alignments (the game's term for natures). Hardy, Docile, Bashful, and Quirky are not valid in this format.

## Serious is a Placeholder

**Serious** is the free neutral alignment — it applies no stat boost or reduction and costs **0 VP**.

In practice, **Serious means "no nature has been chosen yet."** When a Pokemon is first added to a team, it defaults to Serious. Before finalizing a build, the player should almost always assign a real nature that boosts an attacking or defensive stat relevant to the Pokemon's role.

**Do not leave a Pokemon on Serious unless you have a specific reason** (e.g., a pure utility mon where no stat matters more than another). Always ask the user if they want to assign a nature when building a new team member.

## VP Cost

- Serious: **0 VP** (free)
- Any other nature: **500 VP**

## Common Picks by Role

- **Physical sweeper**: Adamant (+Atk/-SpA) or Jolly (+Spe/-SpA)
- **Special sweeper**: Modest (+SpA/-Atk) or Timid (+Spe/-Atk)
- **Trick Room attacker**: Brave (+Atk/-Spe) or Quiet (+SpA/-Spe)
- **Physically bulky support**: Bold (+Def/-Atk) or Impish (+Def/-SpA)
- **Specially bulky support**: Calm (+SpD/-Atk) or Careful (+SpD/-SpA)
- **Speed control / Tailwind support**: Timid or Jolly depending on attacking stat
